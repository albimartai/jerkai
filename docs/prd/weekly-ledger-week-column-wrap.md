## 0. Scope & Sequencing Note (read first)

`app/ui/weekly-ledger.tsx`'s `WeekRowContent` component (read directly, 2026-08-19, lines 59–80) renders every row's leftmost "Week" cell as `<span className="truncate text-xs font-medium uppercase tracking-wide text-zinc-500">{label}</span>`, where `label` is either `` `this week · ${row.daysElapsed} of 7 days` `` (the single in-progress row) or `` `${fmtDay(row.weekStart)}–${fmtDay(row.weekEnd)}` `` (every completed week, e.g. "Jul 13–Jul 19"). Tailwind's `truncate` utility (`overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis; white-space: nowrap`) clips whichever of these doesn't fit the column's width to one line with a trailing ellipsis. The Week column is the first of six in a `grid-cols-[1.3fr_repeat(5,minmax(0,1fr))]` grid (`app/ui/weekly-ledger.tsx:57`) — roughly a fifth of the ledger's `max-w-3xl` width, further narrowed by `uppercase tracking-wide` letter-spacing — so the in-progress label, the longer of the two (up to "this week · 7 of 7 days"), is the one that actually clips in practice; Albert's own example, "this week · 3 of 7 day...", confirms it clips mid-word. The completed-week date-range labels ("Jul 13–Jul 19") are short enough that they do not visibly clip at this width today, and this slice does not change how they render.

**Decision — remove `truncate` (wrap instead), do not add a hover tooltip as the primary fix

What this slice is NOT: not a redesign of the ledger's grid columns or breakpoints; not a change to the completed-week date-range label; not a `title`/tooltip addition (see decision above — filed as an optional, independent fast-follow, not part of this slice); not a change to any of the five metric columns (Body fat, Weight, Strain, Recovery, Lean mass) or their cell-rendering logic.

## 2. Problem statement

`app/ui/weekly-ledger.tsx`'s in-progress-week label (`"this week · N of 7 days"`) is rendered inside a `<span className="truncate ...">`, which clips it with an ellipsis when it doesn't fit the Week column's width — confirmed reproducible today at the column's actual rendered width for any `daysElapsed` value, per Albert's own report ("this week · 3 of 7 day..."). The information after the clip point (how many of the 7 days actually have data) is exactly the information the label exists to convey, and it is currently unrecoverable without resizing the browser window or reading the page's markup directly — there is no hover affordance, no wrap, no alternate access to the clipped text today.

## 3. Objective

The in-progress week's "this week · N of 7 days" label is always fully visible, on any viewport width the app already supports, with no part of it hidden behind an ellipsis. Success = a single testable condition: rendering the ledger's in-progress row produces markup where the label's full text is present and unclipped (no CSS `overflow: hidden`/`text-overflow: ellipsis` applied to that `<span>`).
